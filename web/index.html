Every social network is loud

A poem needs silence around it. Feeds bury it under everything else. Counters turn it into a score. That's the gap.

What Stanza does

You sign in, pick a name, and publish. Each poem gets its own page. Your name gets a page with all of them. The front page is everyone's, newest first. That's it. That's the whole product.

Everywhere

Web, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows and Linux. Same poems, same account.
